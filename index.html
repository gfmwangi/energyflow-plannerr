How to Use EnergyFlow Planner

  1. Log your energy level from 1–10 in the energy section.
  2. Add notes about how you feel or what affected your energy.
  3. Add daily tasks in the planner section.
  4. Check your energy logs to see your productivity patterns.
  5. Use the planner to organize your day based on your energy.
